On every plan

The non-negotiables.

These show up whether you pay us or not. They're not features we meter.

True zero-knowledge

The server stores ciphertext. Only your passphrase can turn it back into files.

Open crypto standards

AES-256-GCM for files, PBKDF2 for keys. Both open, both auditable.

Never scanned

We don't fingerprint, index, or hash your content. Not for compliance, not for anything.

No AI training

Our zero-knowledge model makes it impossible. Our terms say so for metadata too.

Storage in the US

Encrypted drives in US-based facilities. EU facilities land in 2027.

Pay via PayPal

Guest checkout, no PayPal account required. Cancel in two clicks.
