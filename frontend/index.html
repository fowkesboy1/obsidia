How it works

Three steps. One key. Only yours.

The important part happens before anything touches our servers. Your passphrase derives a key that lives on your device. We never see it.

01

Encrypt in your browser

When you drop a file in, your device encrypts it with AES-256-GCM. The key is derived from your passphrase with PBKDF2 — never sent, never stored.

02

Upload ciphertext only

Only encrypted bytes leave your device. To Obsidia, your files are indistinguishable from random noise. We couldn't look if we wanted to.

03

Access from anywhere

Sign in on any device, enter your passphrase, decrypt in-browser. Same files, same key, no cloud ever held the plaintext.
